# 🏏 IPL Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The IPL Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution built to analyze Indian Premier League (IPL) match data across multiple seasons.

The dashboard provides insights into team performance, venue statistics, toss impact, player achievements, and match trends, enabling users to explore historical IPL data through interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw IPL match data into actionable insights by analyzing:

- Match trends across seasons
- Team performance metrics
- Toss impact on match outcomes
- Venue-wise match distribution
- City-wise match analysis
- Player of the Match achievements

---

# 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Total Matches | Total IPL matches played |
| Total Seasons | Number of IPL seasons analyzed |
| Total Teams | Total participating teams |
| Total Venues | Stadiums used for IPL matches |
| Toss Win % | Percentage of matches won after winning the toss |
| Highest Run Margin | Largest victory margin by runs |

# 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

## 1️⃣ Total Matches by Season and Year

**Chart Type:** Line Chart

### Purpose
Shows the trend of IPL matches played across seasons.

### Business Insight
- Identifies growth of the tournament over time.
- Highlights seasons with maximum match activity.
- Tracks expansion of IPL across years.

---

## 2️⃣ Count of Toss Winner by Match Winner

**Chart Type:** Donut Chart

### Purpose
Compares teams winning the toss against match-winning outcomes.

### Business Insight
- Measures toss influence on match results.
- Helps identify teams benefiting most from toss decisions.
- Useful for strategic match analysis.

---

## 3️⃣ Total Matches by City

**Chart Type:** Horizontal Bar Chart

### Purpose
Displays IPL match distribution across cities.

### Business Insight
- Identifies major IPL hosting cities.
- Highlights geographical concentration of matches.
- Useful for venue planning and fan engagement analysis.

---

## 4️⃣ Total Matches by Venue

**Chart Type:** Bar Chart

### Purpose
Shows stadium-wise match distribution.

### Business Insight
- Identifies most frequently used IPL venues.
- Evaluates venue popularity.
- Helps understand infrastructure utilization.

---

## 5️⃣ Total Seasons by Player of the Match

**Chart Type:** Column Chart

### Purpose
Displays top-performing players based on Player of the Match awards.

### Business Insight
- Highlights consistent performers.
- Measures player impact across seasons.
- Useful for player performance evaluation.

---

## 6️⃣ Toss Decision Analysis

**Chart Type:** Pie Chart

### Purpose
Analyzes captain decisions after winning the toss.

### Business Insight
- Compares batting vs fielding preferences.
- Reveals tactical trends in IPL.
- Supports match strategy analysis.

---

## 7️⃣ Total Matches by Winner

**Chart Type:** Column Chart

### Purpose
Compares match victories by teams.

### Business Insight
- Identifies most successful IPL franchises.
- Measures historical team dominance.
- Useful for team performance benchmarking.
